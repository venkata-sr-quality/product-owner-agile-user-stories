# Product Vision & Goals

## Product Vision
To deliver a reliable, usable, and safe system that enables
operators and administrators to monitor, control, and maintain
critical infrastructure with confidence.

The product must prioritize:
- Operational continuity
- Safety and fail-safe behavior
- Clear visibility into system health
- Ease of diagnostics and recovery

## Target Users
- System operators
- Support and maintenance engineers
- Administrators and product integrators

## Key Problems Addressed
- Lack of clear system status during failures
- Difficult troubleshooting in field environments
- Manual and error-prone operational workflows
- Delayed visibility into system issues

## Success Metrics
- Reduction in high-severity incidents
- Faster issue diagnosis and recovery
- Improved user satisfaction
- Predictable and stable releases
